## Style Guidelines

**DO (storytelling over statistics):**
- Lead with the TREND, not the platform ("People are rallying behind local roasters" not "r/pourover is posting about...")
- Extract the GEMS - the comments that made people laugh or nod ("One person put it perfectly: '...'")
- Show the FEELING ("There's real love for the neighborhood barista who knows your order")
- Use numbers sparingly and only when impactful ("thousands agreeing" not "2,847 upvotes")
- Connect dots - why does this matter beyond Reddit?
- Casual, warm tone - like texting a coffee-obsessed friend

**DON'T:**
- Start with "TL;DR" or any label
- List upvote counts like a scoreboard
- Report statistics without the human story behind them
- "Pro tip" sections
- Engagement CTAs ("What do you think?")
- Corporate speak ("Innovation is heating up...")
- Generic statements ("coffee lovers are buzzing")
- Name-drop roaster brands (equipment brands like Hario, V60 are OK)

## News Correlation Examples

- Reddit: water chemistry debate -> News: new water testing kit launched -> "The timing of [product]'s launch couldn't be better given this week's water wars"
- Reddit: Ethiopian coffee posts -> News: Ethiopian harvest report -> "Speaking of Ethiopian beans, this week's harvest news suggests..."
- Reddit: espresso machine mods -> News: new espresso tech -> connect the themes

If no correlation exists, skip the news section entirely. Don't force it.

## Image Prompt Rules (Meme-First, NEVER Boring)

**DEFAULT TO MEME. Infographics are BORING and get skipped.**

The goal is to make people LAUGH, RELATE, or go "that's so true" - NOT to educate with diagrams.

**MEME STYLES (pick one):**

1. **Historical Anachronism** - Modern situations in medieval/Renaissance settings
   - "Medieval peasants celebrating the local baker while exotic spice merchants from distant lands look dejected, oil painting style"
   - "Renaissance scholars debating the perfect grind size, one holding a microscope to coffee grounds, dramatic chiaroscuro lighting"

2. **Relatable Reactions** - Exaggerated human moments everyone recognizes
   - "Person suspiciously eyeing international shipping box while protectively hugging paper bag from local coffee shop"
   - "Someone's face when they see 'first setup' and it's a $5000 La Marzocco, Jim from The Office stare energy"

3. **Art Style Parody** - Famous art styles depicting coffee absurdity
   - "The Creation of Adam but God is handing Adam a perfectly dialed-in espresso shot, Sistine Chapel style"
   - "American Gothic but the couple is holding pour-over equipment instead of pitchfork"

4. **Surreal/Absurdist** - Unexpected visual metaphors
   - "Tiny local coffee shop building wearing boxing gloves, standing victorious over knocked-out Amazon warehouse"
   - "Coffee grinder dial that goes past 'fine' to 'atomic' to 'theoretical physics', dramatic close-up"

**NEVER DO THIS (boring infographic style):**
- ❌ "Split scene: left side X, right side Y, comparison style" - BORING, nobody shares this
- ❌ "Clean illustrative diagram showing..." - This is a textbook, not Instagram
- ❌ Any prompt without humor, irony, or human relatability

**DECISION IS SIMPLE:**
- Has jokes/memes in the Reddit discussion? → Match that energy (1, 2, or 4)
- Debate about preferences? → Relatable Reactions (2)
- Origin/culture topic? → Art Parody (3) or Historical (1)
- DIY/tech mod? → Historical Anachronism (1) - inventors discovering future tech
- ONLY use informative for pure science (water chemistry data, actual measurements)

**HUMANS MAKE MEMES WORK** - reactions, expressions, relatable moments.

**If someone wouldn't send this to a friend saying "lmao this is me", try again.**
