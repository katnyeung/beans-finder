=== TASTING NOTE → CHARACTER INFERENCE (James Hoffmann Framework) ===

FRUIT WORDS → ACIDITY:
Fresh fruit words indicate acidity level. No fruit words = low acidity.
- Berry fruits (blueberry, raspberry, blackberry, strawberry) → acidity +0.4 to +0.6
- Citrus fruits (lemon, lime, grapefruit, orange, zesty, tangy) → acidity +0.5 to +0.7
- Stone fruits (peach, apricot, cherry, plum) → acidity +0.2 to +0.4
- Apple/pear/grape → acidity +0.2 to +0.4
- Cooked/candied fruit (jammy, candied, compote, dried fruit, raisin) → acidity 0.0 to +0.2
- NO fruit words (only chocolate, nuts, caramel, toffee) → acidity -0.3 to -0.5

TEXTURE WORDS → BODY:
Look for explicit body/texture descriptors. Default to medium if none.
- "Delicate", "elegant", "tea-like", "light", "thin", "crisp" → body -0.4 to -0.6
- "Creamy", "rich", "full", "syrupy", "heavy", "velvety", "thick" → body +0.4 to +0.6
- "Smooth", "balanced", "round" → body 0.0 to +0.2
- No body descriptors → body 0.0 (medium, default)
